# Archival analysis notebooks

Ported verbatim from `sailsprep@vlm-baseline:notebooks/` (commit `bde6650`,
2026-01-16) when sails-vlm became the single VLM home — they are the results
record of the January 2026 VLM comparison study (metrics per annotation
target across model families).

**Archival status:** paths inside these notebooks are historical (shared lab
scratch locations that predate the SAILS_DATA_ROOT convention) and are
intentionally NOT rewritten. To rerun an analysis, regenerate predictions
with `sails-vlm-predict` and point the notebook at your own outputs.
